About Me

Building a foundation, one lab at a time.

I'm Sohan Kharel, a BCA student with a genuine, deep pull toward cybersecurity — the kind that has me spinning up a home lab on a weekend instead of putting it off.

I'm currently sharpening my fundamentals in Linux, networking, and Python, while steadily moving into web application security. I learn best by breaking things safely — spinning up vulnerable machines, tearing apart traffic in Wireshark, and documenting every finding like a real engagement.

I care more about understanding why a vulnerability exists than memorizing the fix — that mindset is what I'm building my entire career around.
